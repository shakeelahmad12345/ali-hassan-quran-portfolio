A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Knowledge shared with patience and purpose.

I am an Islamic Studies and Quran teacher with experience teaching children and adults in madrasa and home-tuition settings.

My teaching focuses on Quran with Tajweed and Qira'at, Islamic Studies, and foundational Arabic grammar. My educational background includes Dars-e-Nizami, a B.A., and a two-year course in Tajweed and Qira'at.

Every learner starts from a different place. I work to make lessons clear, welcoming and well paced, so students can build their understanding with confidence.
